🔒 Privacy First — Always

Most tools on ToolX Pro work 100% client-side — meaning your files, text, and data are processed entirely inside your browser and are never sent to any external server. Your data stays on your device.

ℹ️ AI Tools Transparency: The only exceptions are our AI-powered tools (Grammar Fixer and AI Humanizer), which send your input text securely to Google's Gemini AI API for natural language processing. These tools are clearly labeled "AI" on the site. Please review our Privacy Policy for full details.
