🔒 Privacy First — Client-Side Architecture

The majority of tools on ToolX Pro operate 100% client-side using HTML5, Canvas API, WebAssembly, and local JavaScript engines. This guarantees that your sensitive documents, PDFs, photos, and voice audio streams are processed entirely within your device's browser memory and are never uploaded to any remote server.

ℹ️ AI Tools Transparency: AI-powered intelligence features (such as AI Text & Image Detector, AI Humanizer, and Grammar Fixer) communicate securely with Google's Gemini API via encrypted SSL connections exclusively to analyze content and generate outputs. No personal user data, uploaded images, or input text is permanently retained or used for model training. Review our Privacy Policy for comprehensive details.
