❓ Quick Support FAQ

Are all tools on ToolX Pro really 100% free?

Yes, absolutely. All 25+ calculators, image compressors, PDF utilities, and AI helpers are completely free to use with zero hidden subscriptions or paywalls.

Do you store or upload my files to your servers?

No. Almost all tools operate entirely client-side inside your local browser memory using HTML5 and WebAssembly. Your photos, assignments, and PDFs never leave your device.

How can I request a new university cover page format or grading formula?

Simply select "University Grading / Cover Template Request" in the form above and provide your institution name and grading rules. We frequently add community-requested university formats!

How fast will I receive a reply?

We review all incoming inquiries daily and typically respond within 12 to 24 hours.
