Clear and accurate answers about ToolX Pro features, privacy, and utilities.

Is ToolX Pro completely free to use? ▼

Yes! ToolX Pro is 100% free with no subscription fees, usage limits, or hidden paywalls. You can access all our online calculators, AI tools, PDF utilities, image editors, and converters without any restrictions.

How does ToolX Pro protect my privacy and files? ▼

All tools on ToolX Pro operate 100% client-side directly inside your web browser. Your uploaded images, PDFs, text, and calculation inputs are never uploaded to or stored on any external server, ensuring complete data privacy and security.

Do I need to register or create an account? ▼

No registration, email address, or account signup is required. You can use all web utilities completely anonymously and instantly on any device.

How accurate are the student calculators and utility tools on ToolX Pro? ▼

All calculators strictly follow official academic and international standards: the SSC/HSC GPA calculator incorporates the official NCTB 4th subject bonus points rule, the BD University CGPA calculator applies the UGC uniform 4.00 grading scale, and the BMI calculator follows WHO health guidelines.

What categories of tools are available on ToolX Pro? ▼

ToolX Pro features 22+ essential utilities organized into 5 clean categories: 🤖 AI Tools (AI Humanizer & Grammar Fixer), 🎓 Student Corner (Assignment Cover Generator, SSC/HSC Board GPA, University CGPA), 📄 PDF Tools (PDF to Word, PDF Merge, Image to PDF, PDF Compressor, Page Reorder), 🖼️ Image Tools (Image Compressor, Converter, Passport Photo Maker, Background Remover), and 🛠️ Calculators & Utilities (Age Calculator, BMI Calculator, Word Counter, Bangla Unicode Converter, QR Generator & Scanner, Unit Converter, File Size Converter).

Can I use ToolX Pro on mobile devices and offline? ▼

Yes! ToolX Pro is designed mobile-first and works smoothly across all smartphones, tablets, and desktop browsers. Since all processing runs locally inside your browser sandbox, tools continue to work even with poor or intermittent internet connection once loaded.
