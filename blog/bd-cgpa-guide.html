Complete Guide to Calculating GPA & CGPA in Bangladesh

Published on August 19, 2026 • 5 min read

Understanding how Grade Point Average (GPA) and Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) are calculated in Bangladesh can be confusing because the calculation rules differ significantly between school board examinations (SSC/HSC) and University semester systems.

🇧🇩 1. SSC & HSC 4th Subject Rule Explained

In Bangladeshi secondary and higher secondary education boards (Dhaka, Chittagong, Rajshahi, Comilla, etc.), the 4th optional subject provides a bonus boost to your final GPA:

The Official Board Rule:

Any grade points in the 4th subject above 2.00 are added to the total grade point sum of your main subjects.

Added Points = Math.max(0, 4th_Subject_GP - 2.0)

Final GPA is then calculated by dividing the sum by the number of mandatory subjects (Maximum cap is always 5.00).

🎓 2. University CGPA Credit-Weighted Formula

For Bangladeshi universities (National University, Public and Private Universities under the UGC uniform grading system), CGPA is credit-weighted:

CGPA = Σ(Course GPA × Course Credits) / Total Credits Completed
