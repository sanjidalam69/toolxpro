How to Calculate BMI & Understand Results

Published on July 21, 2026 • 5 min read

Body Mass Index, or BMI, is a quick and widely used medical screening tool to check if a person has a healthy ratio of weight to height. While it doesn't measure body fat percentage directly, it correlates strongly with metabolic health risks.

📐 The Mathematical Formula for BMI

Calculating your BMI is straightforward. The formula differs depending on whether you use the metric system or the imperial system:

Metric System (Standard):

BMI = Weight (kg) / [Height (m)]²

Imperial System (US Standard):

BMI = [Weight (lbs) / (Height (in))²] × 703

📊 Understanding BMI Classifications

According to the World Health Organization (WHO), adults over 20 years old are grouped into the following categories based on their BMI score:

BMI Range Classification Health Risk Level
Below 18.5 Underweight Moderate Risk
18.5 – 24.9 Normal Weight Minimal Risk (Healthy)
25.0 – 29.9 Overweight Increased Risk
30.0 and Above Obesity High Clinical Risk

💡 Tips to Maintain a Healthy BMI

  1. Eat a Balanced Diet: Focus on whole foods, vegetables, fruits, lean proteins, and complex carbohydrates. Limit processed sugars.
  2. Stay Physically Active: Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate aerobic exercises (like brisk walking or cycling) every week.
  3. Track Your Metrics: Consistently monitor your progress using online tools to keep tabs on weight changes.
