📑 Streamlining Document Submission with Image to PDF

Converting mobile photos of physical papers into a unified PDF file is an essential workflow for students submitting coursework assignments, remote workers filing expense receipts, and professionals submitting legal documentation for verification portals.

🌟 Best Practices for High-Quality Document Scans

  1. Ensure Even Overhead Lighting: Avoid casting harsh shadows over paper text while photographing with your smartphone.
  2. Align Aspect Ratios: When compiling portrait document pages, select the A4 Portrait option with standard 20px margins for uniform printable output.
  3. Maintain Chronological Order: Upload multi-page lecture notes in sequential order so your final compiled PDF reads naturally from cover to conclusion.
