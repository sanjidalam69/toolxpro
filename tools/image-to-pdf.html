🎨 CamScanner Filter Presets: Choosing the Best Output for Your Document

Different document types require tailored contrast and chromatic curves. You can switch filters globally for all pages or customize each page individually from its dedicated card:

✨ Magic Color (CamScanner Pro Standard - Recommended)

The hallmark CamScanner look. Removes dark shadows, ambient yellow tints, and desk gradients to render the paper background pure snow-white (#FFFFFF). Deepens handwriting, pencil sketches, and ballpoint ink while fully preserving colorful highlights (such as pink margin lines, blue ballpen text, and red grading marks).

📄 B&W Clean Scan (High-Contrast Photocopy)

Simulates a clean Xerox laser photocopy. Binarizes document pixels using adaptive local ratios, forcing all paper background to pure white and printed or written text to stark black. Ideal for legal contracts, government affidavits, and monochrome office printing where file size must remain minimal.

⚡ Sharp Document (Detail & Contrast Boost)

Applies high-frequency edge enhancement without stripping paper texture. Perfect for colored illustrations, university lab graphs, organic chemistry reaction diagrams, and textbook pages containing photographs alongside text.

🩶 Grayscale Scan (Smooth Tonal Reproduction)

Converts images into a balanced 8-bit monochromatic spectrum with gentle S-curve contrast. Preserves fine shading in graphite pencil drawings, architectural blueprints, and engineering schematics.

🖼️ Original (Raw Photographic Pixels)

Bypasses all algorithmic contrast and brightness adjustments, embedding your camera pixels directly into the PDF. Best when photographic authenticity or historical document preservation is required.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How does the Magic Color filter make paper pure white without fading handwriting?

The filter calculates local background luminance across the document using a fast separable box blur. Because all three RGB channels are scaled by identical luminance gain, the paper background is normalized to pure white (#FFFFFF) while handwriting contrast is deepened. Colored inks (like pink margin lines and blue ballpoint ink) retain their natural chromatic hue without any cyan or neon distortion.

Q: How does the tool prevent handwriting, dates, and margin lines from getting cut off?

Our adaptive document detector samples the baseline paper luminance directly from the document center. It distinguishes outer dark surfaces (laptops, desk wood, browser borders) without encroaching upon top date boxes, margin headers, or bottom code lines. Furthermore, our interactive cropper allows millimeter-level manual adjustment with a 2.4x magnifying loupe.

Q: Can I convert multiple images into a single multi-page PDF document?

Yes. You can select and upload dozens of JPG, PNG, or WebP files simultaneously. The tool allows you to reorder pages up or down, rotate individual pages in 90-degree increments, customize filters per page, and merges them into a single chronological PDF.

Q: What is the best page size for printing or online submission?

A4 (Standard 210 × 297 mm) is the universal standard for university assignments, official certificates, and international office printing. Choose Letter (8.5 × 11 in) for North American print requirements, or Auto-Fit if you want each page to match the exact natural aspect ratio of your photographed notebook.

Q: Are my uploaded photos and documents kept safe and private?

Yes, 100% private. All boundary detection, document filtering, and PDF compilation are executed directly inside your local web browser memory using client-side JavaScript. No images or compiled PDF documents are ever uploaded to any remote server or third-party cloud.
