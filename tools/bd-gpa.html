📌 Official 4th Subject Rule: If you get above GP 2.00 (i.e. Grade C) in your 4th subject, the extra points (Grade Point - 2.0) are added to your main total before dividing by the mandatory subject count. A fail in the 4th subject will not fail you overall, but getting an F in any compulsory subject results in an overall Fail.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How does the 4th subject bonus point system work in SSC & HSC?

Under Bangladesh Education Board regulations, the minimum passing grade in the 4th (optional) subject is GP 2.00 (Grade C). Any points earned above 2.00 (e.g. if you get A+ / GP 5.0, your bonus is 5.0 - 2.0 = 3.0 points) are added to your main subject total before dividing by the mandatory subject count.

Q: Can the maximum GPA exceed 5.00 with 4th subject bonus points?

No. The maximum attainable GPA is capped at 5.00 (Golden A+). If the total calculated GPA exceeds 5.00, it automatically normalizes to 5.00.

Q: What happens if a student fails in the 4th subject?

Failing (Grade F / GP 0.0) in the 4th subject does not result in an overall fail for the student. However, no bonus points will be added to the overall score.
