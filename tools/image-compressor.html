⚙️ Technical Breakdown of Supported Image Formats

📷 JPEG / JPG

Best suited for complex camera photographs containing rich gradients and millions of colors. Uses discrete cosine transform (DCT) lossy compression.

🎨 PNG

Ideal for logos, screenshots, and icons requiring alpha-channel transparency and sharp text contrast.

⚡ WebP

Google's modern web container that delivers 25%–34% smaller file sizes than comparable JPEGs at identical visual index scores.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do I reduce an image under 100 KB or 200 KB for government or job application forms?

Upload your image and adjust the quality slider to approximately 50% - 65%. Click "Compress Image" and check the resulting file size. If still above your threshold, slide down to 40% and re-compress until it meets the exact portal requirement.

Q: Does ToolX Pro store copies of my personal photos?

No. The entire process runs in your client-side browser memory via HTML5 Canvas. No image data is sent across the internet, ensuring 100% privacy for confidential documents, IDs, and family photos.
