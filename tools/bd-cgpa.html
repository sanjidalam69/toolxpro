📋 UGC Bangladesh Uniform Grading Scale (4.00 Scale)

Standardized grading template formulated by University Grants Commission (UGC) of Bangladesh:

Numerical Marks Letter Grade Grade Point (GP) Assessment / Classification
80% and Above A+ 4.00 Outstanding (First Class)
75% to <80% A 3.75 Excellent (First Class)
70% to <75% A- 3.50 Very Good (First Class)
65% to <70% B+ 3.25 Good (First Class)
60% to <65% B 3.00 Satisfactory (First Class Minimum)
55% to <60% B- 2.75 Above Average (Second Class)
50% to <55% C+ 2.50 Average (Second Class)
45% to <50% C 2.25 Below Average (Second Class Minimum)
40% to <45% D 2.00 Pass (Third Class)
Less than 40% F 0.00 Fail
🥇 First Class Equivalent: CGPA 3.00 to 4.00 (60% to 100%)
🥈 Second Class Equivalent: CGPA 2.25 to 2.99 (45% to 59%)
🥉 Third Class / Pass: CGPA 2.00 to 2.24 (40% to 44%)
