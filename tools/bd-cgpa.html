❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What is the official formula for calculating CGPA in Bangladesh?

CGPA is the credit-weighted average: CGPA = Σ(Course Credits × Grade Points) / Σ(Total Course Credits). Each subject's contribution is directly proportional to its credit hours.

Q: Does National University (NU) follow the same scale as Public & Private universities?

Yes. The University Grants Commission (UGC) Bangladesh uniform grading scale is followed by National University (NU), Public Universities (DU, RU, JU, CU, BUET), and all approved Private Universities.

Q: What minimum CGPA is required for First Class equivalence?

According to Ministry of Education guidelines, a CGPA of 3.00 out of 4.00 is officially equivalent to First Class (60%+ marks) for BCS, government recruitment, and higher education admissions.
