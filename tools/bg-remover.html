🔬 Understanding Alpha Channels and PNG Transparency

Standard JPEG images store color across three 8-bit channels: Red (R), Green (G), and Blue (B), producing an opaque 24-bit canvas. Transparent PNG files introduce a fourth 8-bit channel called the Alpha Channel (A), yielding a 32-bit RGBA pixel space:

  • Alpha Value 255 (100% Opaque): Pixels belonging strictly to the foreground subject remain completely solid and visible.
  • Alpha Value 0 (100% Transparent): Pixels classified as background are cleared to zero opacity, revealing whatever background surface the image is later placed on.
  • Sub-Pixel Feathering (Values 1 to 254): Boundary pixels along soft contours (such as hair or garment borders) receive gradual semi-transparency to eliminate harsh pixelated jaggedness and color halos.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Is my personal photo uploaded or stored on any external server?

No. 100% of the neural segmentation, color matrix computation, and pixel erasing occurs locally inside your web browser via WebGL and WebAssembly. Your photos, private signatures, and documents never leave your physical device.

Q: How do I remove a white background from a photographed signature?

Upload your signature photo, select Color Key / Click Sampler from the tool dropdown, and click anywhere on the white paper in the preview canvas. Set tolerance between 12% and 18% to erase the paper while keeping the ink strokes sharp.

Q: Why does a downloaded PNG show a black background when viewed in some galleries?

Some default smartphone image viewers render transparent alpha pixels against a solid black or dark gray backdrop by default. When you insert the downloaded PNG into Word, Canva, PowerPoint, or web pages, the transparency functions correctly without any background color.

Q: What is the maximum image resolution supported?

ToolX Pro supports full high-resolution images up to 4K resolution (4096×4096 px). Performance depends on your device's GPU capabilities. For extremely fast processing on low-end smartphones, images between 1080p and 2K are ideal.

Q: Can I remove backgrounds from animal or non-human photos?

The AI Selfie model is specifically tuned for human subjects. For non-human objects, animals, or products photographed against single-color backgrounds, switch to the Color Key / Chroma Sampler mode, which works on any object regardless of category.
