🌐 Metric (SI) vs Imperial Measurement Systems

The International System of Units (SI Metric) is decimal-based and used across global science, engineering, and commerce. In contrast, the US Customary / Imperial system is standard in North America. Converting between these systems with mathematical precision is essential for construction, cooking, navigation, and international shipping.

🌡️ Temperature Conversion Formulas

Celsius to Fahrenheit:

°F = (°C × 1.8) + 32

Fahrenheit to Celsius:

°C = (°F - 32) / 1.8

Celsius to Kelvin:

K = °C + 273.15

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How many centimeters are in an inch?

By international standard treaty definition, exactly 1 inch = 2.54 centimeters.

Q: Does the calculator calculate floating-point decimals accurately?

Yes. The conversion engine uses IEEE 754 floating-point standards with automated rounding to deliver clean, precise numbers without binary floating artifacts.
