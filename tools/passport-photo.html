🛂 Official Passport & Visa Photo Guidelines

Government immigration departments (such as the US Department of State, UK Visas and Immigration, and the Department of Immigration & Passports Bangladesh) enforce strict biometric specifications. Submitting non-compliant photographs with incorrect head ratios, shadowed backgrounds, or improper dimensions is the leading cause of visa delays.

✅ Essential Checklist for 100% Approval

  1. Pose Directly Toward the Camera: Maintain full frontal facial view with both ears visible and shoulders level.
  2. Neutral Facial Expression: Keep both eyes open, mouth closed, and avoid exaggerated smiles or frowns.
  3. Eyeglasses & Headwear: Eyeglasses are prohibited in US and modern biometric visas to eliminate glare. Religious head coverings are permitted if they do not obscure facial contours.
  4. Eliminate Shadows: Ensure balanced lighting across your forehead and cheekbones to prevent background shadows.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How does the AI Auto-Fit feature work?

Our local AI detects your eye level and facial landmarks using MediaPipe. It calculates head tilt, straightens your posture to perfect 0° horizontal alignment, and scales the photo so your head occupies the exact 70%–80% biometric space required by government authorities.

Q: How do I print the downloaded A4 or 4x6 sheet?

Open the downloaded PDF or PNG and choose "Print". In your printer settings dialog, ensure scaling is set to "100%" or "Actual Size" (do not select "Fit to Printable Area") to ensure exact physical millimeter dimensions.

Q: Does my photo ever leave my device?

No! 100% of face landmark detection, AI background cutout, and 300 DPI rendering occur locally in your browser using HTML5 Canvas and client-side WebAssembly. Your photos are never uploaded to remote servers.
