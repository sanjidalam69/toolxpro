⏱️ How Estimated Reading Time Works

Reading speed varies among individuals, but cognitive research indicates that the average literate adult reads English at approximately 200 to 250 words per minute (WPM) for non-technical material. For spoken audio (such as podcasts, speeches, or video scripts), the standard comfortable speaking pace is 130 to 150 words per minute.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: What is the difference between character count with and without spaces?

"Characters with spaces" includes every letter, number, punctuation mark, whitespace, and newline. "Characters without spaces" (No Spaces) counts only visible typographic glyphs, which is often used in freelance copywriting rate calculations.

Q: How are sentence boundaries detected?

Sentences are identified by standard terminal punctuation marks: periods (.), exclamation marks (!), and question marks (?), excluding common abbreviations like "e.g.", "Dr.", or "U.S.".

Q: Is there any limit to the amount of text I can paste?

No limit. You can paste entire book chapters, dissertation manuscripts, or large code files. Processing is executed instantly inside your local browser memory.
