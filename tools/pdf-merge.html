❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Will merged PDF pages maintain their original orientation?

Yes. The merger respects individual page rotation metadata. Portrait and landscape pages coexist seamlessly within the merged output.

Q: Does ToolX Pro view my personal contract files?

No. The merger runs completely offline inside your browser environment. Your files are never sent over the internet or indexed by third parties.
