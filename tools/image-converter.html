🔄 Choosing the Right Image Format for Your Needs

Digital image formats serve different engineering and aesthetic purposes. Selecting the correct file extension prevents unwanted compression artifacts, preserves transparency, and avoids upload rejections on formal application portals.

Format Compression Type Transparency Support Ideal Use Case
JPEG / JPG Lossy (DCT) No (Solid Background) Camera photos, wallpaper, email attachments
PNG Lossless (Deflate) Yes (Alpha Channel) Logos, UI badges, charts, screenshots with text
WebP Lossy & Lossless Yes (Alpha Channel) High-speed websites, eCommerce product catalogs
