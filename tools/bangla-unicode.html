🔤 Unicode vs Bijoy ANSI Encoding in Bengali Typography

Bengali digital typography uses two major character encoding paradigms: standard international UTF-8 Unicode and legacy ANSI ASCII font encodings. Understanding how to transition between them is essential for publishers, graphic designers, newspaper editors, and government document typists.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How do I use converted Bijoy text in Adobe Photoshop?

1. Paste your Unicode text and click "Unicode to Bijoy ANSI ⬇️". 2. Click "Copy Bijoy". 3. Open Adobe Photoshop, select the Text Tool, choose the "SutonnyMJ" font, and paste the clipboard content.

Q: Why does Bijoy text look like gibberish in English web browsers?

Bijoy ANSI maps Bengali glyphs over standard Latin ASCII character slots. When viewed with standard English fonts (Arial, Times New Roman), it renders as Latin characters. It requires the SutonnyMJ font to display as Bengali letters.
