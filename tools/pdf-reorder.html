✂️ Cleaning and Restructuring Multi-Page PDF Files

Automatic document feeders (ADF) on office scanners often capture documents out of order or include unwanted blank separator sheets. Reorganizing and pruning unnecessary pages produces a streamlined, professional document suitable for clients and examination boards.

📋 Typical Scenarios for PDF Page Reordering

  1. Duplex Scan Fixes: When two-sided scanning captures odd pages (1, 3, 5) followed by even pages (6, 4, 2), enter the interleaved sequence to restore natural reading order.
  2. Removing Blank Divider Pages: Easily strip out blank trailing pages generated during scanner feeds.
  3. Extracting Executive Summaries: Create concise summary packets by extracting only key pages (e.g. pages 1, 2, and 12) from large annual reports.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Can I duplicate a page within the document?

Yes. Entering a sequence such as "1, 2, 2, 3" will duplicate page 2 twice in the resulting PDF output.

Q: Is my document data sent to external cloud servers?

No. The rearrangement logic runs 100% locally within your browser sandbox via client-side WebAssembly.
