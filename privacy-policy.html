Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ToolX Pro ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website at toolxpro.live.

By accessing or using ToolX Pro, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our website.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect (Client-Side Tools)

The overwhelming majority of tools on ToolX Pro — including all PDF tools, image compressors, converters, financial & health calculators, student calculators, text utilities, and converters — operate entirely inside your browser via client-side JavaScript. This means:

  • Files you upload (PDFs, images, documents) are never uploaded or transmitted to our servers or any third-party storage.
  • Text, numbers, or parameters you input are never tracked, logged, or stored remotely.
  • All computation happens locally within your device's memory sandbox.
🛡️ Zero Data Retention: These client-side tools have zero persistent data collection. As soon as you reload or close your browser tab, all session calculations and uploaded files are instantly deleted from your temporary local memory.

2. AI-Powered Writing, Vision & Voice Tools

Our AI utilities (currently AI Text & Image Detector, AI Text Humanizer, AI Grammar & Spell Fixer, and Bangla Voice Typing & Speech Translator) provide enhanced language assistance and content verification:

  • For AI text detection, proofreading, and humanizing, the text snippet or image payload submitted is transmitted securely over encrypted SSL connections using Google's Gemini AI API.
  • Image forensics and texture analyses execute client-side using browser canvas processors and transient encrypted vision API calls.
  • Voice typing operates directly through your browser's native Web Speech API and local punctuation engines.
  • We do not store, log, sell, or retain any text, images, or voice inputs you submit to our tools on our servers.

For comprehensive details on Google's privacy and API data handling, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Gemini API Terms of Service
⚠️ User Recommendation: Please do not enter sensitive passwords, financial credentials, or private confidential data into online AI utilities.

5. Cookie Policy

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to ensure proper website functionality. ToolX Pro uses cookies primarily for:

You can configure your browser to block or alert you about cookies. Note that disabling cookies will not prevent our client-side tools from functioning normally.
