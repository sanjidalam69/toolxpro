Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ToolX Pro ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website at toolxpro.live.

By accessing or using ToolX Pro, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our website.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect (Client-Side Tools)

The overwhelming majority of tools on ToolX Pro — including all PDF tools, image compressors, converters, financial & health calculators, text utilities, and BD Specials — operate entirely inside your browser via client-side JavaScript. This means:

  • Files you upload (PDFs, images, documents) are never uploaded or transmitted to our servers or any third-party storage.
  • Text, numbers, or parameters you input are never tracked, logged, or stored remotely.
  • All computation happens locally within your device's memory sandbox.
🛡️ Zero Data Retention: These client-side tools have zero persistent data collection. As soon as you reload or close your browser tab, all session calculations and uploaded files are instantly deleted from your temporary local memory.

2. AI-Powered Tools — Google Gemini API

Our AI Tools (currently AI Grammar & Spell Fixer and AI Text Humanizer) work differently from our standard client-side utilities. When you interact with these tools:

  • The text snippet you submit is sent securely over HTTPS to Google's Gemini AI API for natural language processing and grammar correction.
  • The generated output is transmitted back to your browser and displayed instantly on screen.
  • We do not store, log, sell, or retain any text you submit to our AI tools on our own database or servers.

However, please note that Google receives your input text as part of the API interaction. Google's free-tier API services may process this data according to their terms. For comprehensive details on Google's data handling, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Gemini API Terms of Service
⚠️ Important User Recommendation: Please do not enter sensitive, proprietary, confidential, financial, or personally identifiable information (PII) into the AI-powered tools.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Networks

To keep all 29+ tools on ToolX Pro 100% free for everyone, we display digital advertisements provided by reputable ad networks such as Adsterra and Google AdSense.

These advertising partners may use cookies, web beacons, and similar technologies to:

  • Serve personalized or contextual advertisements relevant to your browsing interests.
  • Collect aggregated, non-personally identifiable usage information (such as browser type, operating system, approximate geographic country/region, and device viewport).
  • Measure the performance and reach of advertising campaigns.

You can opt out of personalized or interest-based advertising at any time by visiting the Digital Advertising Alliance consumer choice tool at aboutads.info/choices or youronlinechoices.com. You may also review Adsterra's Privacy Policy.

4. Web Analytics

We may utilize anonymized, privacy-conscious web analytics tools (such as Google Analytics 4) to monitor website performance, understand user traffic distribution, identify popular tools, and optimize page load speeds.

These analytics tools collect data such as page views, referrer URLs, session duration, and device category. No personally identifiable information (such as names, phone numbers, or email addresses) is captured.

5. Cookie Policy

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to ensure proper website functionality and deliver advertising. ToolX Pro uses cookies primarily for:

  • Theme & Preference: Storing your selected UI theme (Light/Dark mode) in your local storage.
  • Advertising & Delivery: Facilitating non-intrusive ad delivery via our advertising partners.

You can configure your browser to block or alert you about cookies. Note that disabling cookies will not prevent our client-side tools from functioning normally.

We have no control over and assume no responsibility for the content, privacy policies, or practices of any third-party sites. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any site you visit.

ToolX Pro is designed for general audiences and is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children. If you believe a child has submitted personal information through our AI tools, please reach out to us immediately so we can assist.
