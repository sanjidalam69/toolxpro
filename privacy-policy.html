Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ToolX Pro ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website at toolxpro.live.

By accessing or using ToolX Pro, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use our website.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect (100% Client-Side & Local Processing)

The overwhelming majority of tools on ToolX Pro — including all PDF tools, image compressors, converters, background removers, cover page generators (Assignment & Lab Report), financial & health calculators, student board & university GPA/CGPA calculators, and text utilities — operate 100% locally inside your browser via client-side JavaScript, WebAssembly, and HTML5 Canvas. This means:

  • Files you upload or select (PDFs, images, documents, university logos) are never uploaded or transmitted to our servers or any third-party cloud storage.
  • Personal details & academic inputs (student names, student IDs, course titles, teacher names, grades, marks) stay strictly within your browser's private sandbox.
  • All rendering & computation happens locally within your device's memory.
🛡️ Zero Server Uploads & Zero Retention: These client-side tools have zero persistent data collection. As soon as you reload or close your browser tab, all session calculations, generated cover sheets, and uploaded files are instantly erased from your temporary local memory.

2. AI-Powered Writing, Vision & Voice Tools (Cloudflare Edge AI & Google Gemini)

Our specialized AI utilities (currently AI Text & Image Detector, AI Text Humanizer, AI Grammar & Spell Fixer, and Bangla Voice Typing & Speech Translator) provide advanced language assistance, tone enhancement, and content verification:

  • Encrypted Edge AI Transmission: When you submit text or image analysis requests, payloads are transmitted securely over TLS 1.3 encrypted HTTPS connections to our stateless Cloudflare Edge AI Workers and Google Gemini API infrastructure.
  • Zero Data Logging & Zero Retention: Neither ToolX Pro nor our Cloudflare Edge proxies log, store, archive, or inspect your text submissions, uploaded images, or voice transcripts. Requests are processed transiently in-memory and discarded immediately upon returning the result to your browser.
  • Zero Model Training on User Data: ToolX Pro accesses enterprise developer API endpoints with strict data-exclusion policies. Your submitted essays, reports, or articles are never used to train public machine learning or foundation models.
  • Client-Side Vision & Forensics: Image forensic analyses, canvas noise extractions, and texture assessments run directly inside your browser's WebGL / HTML5 Canvas memory before any transient verification check.
  • Local Speech Processing: Voice typing operates natively via the browser's local Web Speech API and client-side punctuation normalization algorithms.

For comprehensive third-party infrastructure terms and privacy safeguards, you may review:

  • Cloudflare Privacy & Security Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Gemini API Terms of Service
🛡️ Zero Logging Guarantee: All AI processing requests are stateless. As soon as the analysis or paraphrase finishes, the data ceases to exist on the edge network.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites on the internet.

  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting aboutads.info/choices.

We may utilize anonymized, privacy-conscious web analytics tools to monitor website uptime, performance, popular tools, and page load speeds.

These analytics tools collect aggregated data such as page views, referrer URLs, session duration, and device category. No personally identifiable information (such as names, phone numbers, or passwords) is captured.

5. Cookie Policy

Cookies are small text files placed on your device to ensure proper website functionality. ToolX Pro uses cookies primarily for:

You can configure your browser to block or alert you about cookies. Note that disabling cookies will not prevent our client-side tools from functioning normally.
