Enterprise-Grade PDF Processing with Zero Data Logging

Standard cloud PDF editors require uploading confidential resumes, bank statements, and legal agreements to remote third-party servers. ToolX Pro executes all PDF rendering, page restructuring, and compression directly inside your device's browser memory via WebAssembly (Wasm) and HTML5 Canvas APIs. Your documents never touch our servers.

🔒 100% Client-Side Memory Processing

PDF files are processed directly within your browser's private sandbox. Sensitive financial records, NID copies, and job applications remain strictly on your personal device at all times.

📉 Smart Vector & Raster Compression

Our compression algorithm optimizes embedded raster imagery down to standard 150 DPI email and portal thresholds while maintaining razor-sharp vector typography for clean printing.

⚡ Instant Processing Without Queues

Since operations do not depend on upload or download queues to cloud servers, merging large multi-page reports and compiling image portfolios happens almost instantaneously.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Is there any file size limit for merging or compressing PDFs?

Because processing utilizes your local device RAM rather than a shared server threshold, you can merge and compress multi-megabyte documents without arbitrary paywalls or queue restrictions.

Q: Will PDF to Word conversion preserve my Bengali text and fonts?

Yes. Standard Unicode Bangla text streams are extracted cleanly with formatting tags, preserving paragraph breaks, headers, and bulleted lists.
