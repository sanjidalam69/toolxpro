Why Use ToolX Pro Student Utilities?

Academic life in Bangladesh requires balancing complex course requirements, strict submission formatting guidelines, and accurate GPA/CGPA calculations. ToolX Pro's Student Corner provides free, zero-login, browser-based tools specifically calibrated for Bangladeshi educational institutions—from secondary education boards (SSC/HSC) to the University Grants Commission (UGC) unified 4.00 grading scale.

🇧🇩 Official 4th Subject Bonus Calculation

Under Bangladesh Board of Intermediate and Secondary Education regulations, any grade points earned above 2.00 in the optional 4th subject are added as bonus points to the total GPA, capped at a maximum of 5.00. Our calculator models this logic with 100% precision.

🎓 UGC Unified 4.00 CGPA Framework

Calculate Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) weighted by course credit hours across semesters. Fully aligned with National University (NU), Dhaka University (DU) affiliated colleges, and private universities operating under UGC Bangladesh guidelines.

🖨️ A4 Print-Ready Submission Standards

Generate pixel-perfect Assignment and Lab Report cover pages formatted for standard ISO A4 paper. Includes dedicated fields for course code, experiment number, submission deadlines, and instructor grading rubrics.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: Can I use the assignment cover generator for any university in Bangladesh?

Yes! The Assignment Cover Generator supports universal university templates where you can upload your custom institution logo, enter your department, course teacher's designation, and student ID.

Q: Does my academic data get saved on your servers?

No. All calculations, grades, names, and cover page designs are processed 100% locally in your web browser. Nothing is transmitted to or stored on external servers.
