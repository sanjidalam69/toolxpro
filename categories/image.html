Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q: How does ToolX Pro guarantee my personal photo privacy?

All transformations (compression, background removal, face detection, and passport framing) run 100% inside your device's browser memory using WebAssembly. Your photos are never transmitted over the internet.

Q: What is the required photo size for BCS and Bangladesh Government job applications?

Government Teletalk application portals strictly mandate a 300 × 300 pixel photo under 100 KB with a solid light background, and a 300 × 80 pixel signature under 60 KB. Both can be configured effortlessly here.

Q: How does client-side compression achieve smaller file sizes without blurring images?

ToolX Pro uses advanced discrete cosine transform (DCT) quantisation and chroma subsampling algorithms. These techniques intelligently strip out color variations that are imperceptible to the human eye while preserving edge sharpness, high-contrast text lines, and facial details.

Q: What is the official photo size for Bangladesh government job applications?

Standard Bangladesh civil service (BPSC / Teletalk) applications require a passport photo measuring exactly 300x300 pixels (file size under 100 KB) and a scanned signature measuring exactly 300x80 pixels (file size under 60 KB). ToolX Pro's Passport Photo tool offers one-click presets for these exact dimensions.
